What Omni-Scope cannot do

  • Very thick wards interfere with readings.
  • Some enchanted materials distort signals.
  • Strong magical interference reduces accuracy.
  • Extreme cold lowers thermal reliability.
  • It detects anomalies, but cannot say exactly what they are.
  • It gives probabilities and alerts, not certainty.
  • It cannot see through everything.
